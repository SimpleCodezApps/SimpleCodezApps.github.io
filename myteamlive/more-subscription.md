Review your subscription, renewal state, and expiration date. The expiration field shows when access renews or ends.

- Tap **View Subscription Options** to start a plan if you are not active yet.
- Tap **Manage Subscription** to change or cancel an existing plan in the App Store.
- Tap **Restore Purchases** if a plan you already bought is not showing up—after reinstalling the app or switching to a new device.

See [Pricing](/myteamlive/pricing) for broadcaster subscription plans and prices.

## Payment issues

If the App Store cannot charge your payment method at renewal, this screen shows a **Payment issue** warning along with the date your access ends. **You keep streaming during this time**—the App Store retries the payment for a few days first.

Tap **Update Payment Method** to fix your card or billing details in the App Store. Once the payment goes through, the warning clears on its own and your subscription renews normally.

If the retries run out before you update your payment method, the warning changes to **Payment failed** and livestreaming is locked until the payment succeeds. Your plan is not canceled—updating your payment method restores access.

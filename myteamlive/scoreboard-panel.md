Adjust scores and sport-specific info.

## Generic Scorebug
- Home and Away steppers adjust scores.

## Hockey Scorebug
- **Shots on Goal** steppers track shots.
- **Period** selects P1, P2, P3, OT, or SO (shootout).
- **Game Over** toggle to show score as Final

If the **Show Clock** option is disabled:

- **Power Play** selects a side for a power play.
- **Player Advantage** select the on-ice strength.
- **Empty Net** indicates which team has pulled their goalie. An empty net adds an extra skater for that team.
- **Skaters** sets the base number of skaters (5v5, 4v4, or 3v3).

## Soccer Controls
- **Period** selects 1H, 2H, ET1, ET2, or SO (penalty shootout).
- **Red Cards** track red cards for each team.

## Shootout
Setting the Hockey or Soccer **Period** to **SO** replaces the power play controls (hockey) or red cards (soccer) with shootout controls.

- Each team's row of circles shows its attempts: green for a score, red for a miss, gray for not yet taken.
- **Score** and **Miss** record the team's next attempt.
- The undo arrow removes the team's last attempt.
- Once a team has won, **Home Wins** or **Away Wins** appears and Score and Miss are disabled.
- **Reset Shootout** clears all attempts.

Use the interactive help demo in the app to experiment without affecting a live game.

- Share your YouTube video link before going live: tap Get Link on the Go Live summary, then Share. The link is also on the Camera Status panel and the remote control's info sheet
- YouTube broadcasts now default to Unlisted instead of Private, and a saved Private setting changes to Unlisted once. Anyone with an Unlisted video's link can watch it; choose Private again on the Go Live screen to keep a broadcast to your channel
- Instant replay keeps working after you switch to another app and come back while live
- A saved recording keeps going after you switch to another app and come back while live, in a new part you can combine with the first
- Minor UI fixes
- Help updates

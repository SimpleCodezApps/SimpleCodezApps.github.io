- Add Instant Replay to mark and play back recent game action without stopping your broadcast
- Add duration-specific replay markers (10s/15s/20s/25s/30s) in Advanced mode
- Automatically tag replays with nearby scoreboard events
- Saved replays show the period and offer 1x and 0.5x playback buttons
- Add a thermal warning indicator to the control bar
- Team colors are now Primary and Alternate, with a per-team choice of scorebug background
- Preview the scorebug in the team editor and swap team colors with one tap
- Soccer elapsed timer keeps counting minutes past 60 instead of rolling over to hours
- Help updates

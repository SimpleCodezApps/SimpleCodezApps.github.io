- Add an optional 5 second stop delay: pressing Stop shows the pause screen and mutes audio while a countdown runs, giving your stream a clean ending (on by default, set on the Go Live screen or in Control Options)
- Show a countdown on screen during the last seconds of an instant replay
- Fix the broadcast sometimes not returning to a live camera after a replay when a remote camera disconnected
- Show a clear message and a Close button when the camera or stream can't be set up, on both the broadcaster and remote cameras
- Reduce audio distortion on loud sounds
- Keep audio steady when switching microphones during a broadcast
- Help updates
